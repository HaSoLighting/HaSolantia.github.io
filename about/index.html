About HaSolantia

HaSolantia is an educational and portfolio platform focused on electrical power systems, power quality, architectural lighting, engineering design, and practical technical learning.

Purpose

The platform brings together technical explanations, engineering projects, design examples, research, and practical learning resources across electrical engineering and architectural lighting.

Mission

To share clear technical knowledge and portfolio-quality project documentation that helps people understand electrical engineering, power-quality challenges, lighting design principles, and practical design workflows.

Vision (Future)

To build a trusted platform and future venture that supports reliable power and better lighting outcomes, especially in environments where infrastructure challenges, technical knowledge gaps, and affordability constraints are significant.
