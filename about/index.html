About Us

Technical Expertise. Practical Solutions.

HaSolantia LLC is a technical design and consulting practice specializing in architectural lighting, photometric analysis, research, and engineering design support for the built environment.

Who We Are

HaSolantia works with architects, designers, businesses, project teams, researchers, and other clients who need technically informed design support, simulation, analysis, research, or documentation.

Our approach brings together design thinking, engineering analysis, simulation, research, and data-driven evaluation to support clear and practical project decisions.

Our Mission

To provide practical, evidence-based lighting and technical design solutions that combine thoughtful design, engineering analysis, and clear communication.

Our Vision

To grow into a multidisciplinary design and technical consulting practice bringing together lighting designers, engineers, researchers, and other specialists to create better-performing built environments.

Our Approach

Analysis That Supports Better Design Decisions

Good technical work should not only produce calculations. It should help project teams understand performance, compare alternatives, identify potential issues, and make informed design decisions.

◇

Technical Rigor

Design decisions are supported by appropriate calculations, simulation, research, applicable standards, and clearly documented assumptions.

◈

Design Integration

Technical performance is considered alongside architecture, visual experience, comfort, energy use, and the overall design intent of the project.

◎

Clear Communication

Findings are communicated through reports, calculations, drawings, visualizations, schedules, and other documentation appropriate to the project.

⟷

Collaborative Delivery

We work with clients and project teams throughout the design process and adapt the scope of our services to the needs of each engagement.

Leadership

Building an Interdisciplinary Practice

Yahya Sogodok

Founder

HaSolantia was founded by Yahya Sogodok, whose interdisciplinary background brings together architectural lighting, electrical engineering, building science, technical research, simulation, and data analysis.

His experience includes architectural lighting design and simulation, lighting research, engineering analysis, technical documentation, and research-driven evaluation of building and lighting systems.

Growing Beyond a Single Discipline

HaSolantia is being developed as a collaborative practice rather than an individual portfolio.

As the company grows, projects may bring together additional lighting designers, engineers, researchers, visualization specialists, and other technical professionals based on project needs.

This structure allows the practice to expand its technical capabilities while maintaining clear project scopes and professional responsibilities.
