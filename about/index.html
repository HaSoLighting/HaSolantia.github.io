About

Portfolio, research interests, and future vision

This website presents academic work, independent design projects, and educational material. HaSolantia is a future venture under development and is not currently offering services, accepting clients, or conducting commercial activity through this site.

The focus areas include electrical power quality (voltage stability, harmonics, grounding and protection concepts) and lighting design (technical design thinking, documentation, and visual comfort principles).

Mission (Educational)

To share clear technical knowledge and portfolio-quality project documentation that helps people understand power-quality challenges and lighting design principles.

Vision (Future)

To build a trusted platform and future venture that supports reliable power and better lighting outcomes, especially in environments where infrastructure challenges and affordability constraints are significant.
