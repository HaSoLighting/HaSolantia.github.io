Design Study

Retail Lighting Design Study

A retail lighting study exploring how lighting composition, intensity, distribution, and architectural integration can influence the perception of spaciousness and closure while supporting visual performance within a sales environment.

Project Overview
Project Snapshot
  • Project type: Academic Design Study
  • Date: June 2024
  • Primary focus: Retail lighting, spaciousness, closure, visual hierarchy, and lighting performance
  • Tools: AGi32, AutoCAD, and manufacturer photometric files
  • Scope: Lighting concept development, simulation, performance evaluation, luminaire application, and documentation
  • Deliverables: Lighting layouts, calculation grids, renderings, fixture selection, and design narrative

View Project Report

Design Objective

Exploring two contrasting spatial experiences

The study examined how different lighting conditions could reinforce contrasting psychological impressions within the same retail environment.

Scene 01
Spaciousness

The spaciousness strategy used higher-intensity, more uniform illumination together with peripheral wall lighting to create a brighter, more open, and visually expansive retail environment.

  • Higher ambient illumination
  • Greater lighting uniformity
  • Peripheral wall illumination
  • Reduced contrast in general areas
  • Cove lighting supporting spatial perception
Scene 02
Closure

The closure strategy used lower-intensity, more non-uniform lighting and reduced peripheral illumination to produce a more intimate and visually contained atmosphere.

  • Lower ambient illumination
  • Increased variation in luminance
  • Limited peripheral lighting
  • Greater visual contrast
  • Accent-focused presentation
Lighting Strategy

Layered lighting for retail function and experience

The design combined multiple lighting layers to support merchandise presentation, visual tasks, circulation, architectural character, and the intended psychological effect of each lighting scene.

General Lighting
Ambient Illumination

2' × 2' panel luminaires provided the principal ambient illumination and established the general lighting level throughout the space.

Merchandise
Accent & Display Lighting

Track lighting was used to emphasize merchandise, strengthen visual hierarchy, and create contrast between display areas and surrounding surfaces.

Architectural Lighting
Cove & Peripheral Lighting

Cove and peripheral lighting were incorporated selectively to reinforce spatial perception, particularly within the spaciousness lighting scene.

Technical Evaluation

Performance considerations

The design was evaluated not only for appearance, but also for measurable lighting performance and practical retail requirements.

Illuminance & Uniformity

Horizontal illumination and distribution were evaluated to support general retail activity, circulation, displays, and visual tasks.

Visual Comfort

Luminaire placement, contrast, brightness, and potential glare were considered in relation to customer and employee visual comfort.

Energy & Sustainability

Lighting power, luminaire selection, distribution, and overall system efficiency were considered as part of the design evaluation.

Color Quality

Color rendering and appearance were considered to support accurate merchandise presentation and a visually appropriate retail environment.

Staff Lighting Needs

The design also considered the lighting needs of sales personnel who may spend extended periods within the retail environment.

Task Lighting

Counter and transaction areas were considered separately to ensure appropriate illumination for detailed visual tasks.
