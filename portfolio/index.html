Project Approach

From project information to useful technical deliverables

Although the scope varies by project, our work generally follows a structured process focused on understanding, analysis, evaluation, and communication.

01

Define

Review project drawings, requirements, objectives, constraints, available data, and applicable criteria.

02

Analyze

Develop the appropriate simulation, calculation, technical study, research method, or analytical model.

03

Evaluate

Compare results with project objectives, design criteria, standards, performance targets, and available alternatives.

04

Communicate

Present findings through clear reports, drawings, calculations, schedules, visualizations, and recommendations.

Project Classification
