Project Process

A clear approach from scope to delivery

Each engagement begins with a defined scope and continues through structured analysis, evaluation, documentation, and project communication.

01

Define the Need

Review the project type, available drawings, technical requirements, requested deliverables, and schedule.

02

Confirm the Scope

Establish the project inputs, responsibilities, deliverables, limitations, timing, and fee.

03

Develop the Analysis

Complete the appropriate calculations, simulation, research, evaluation, or technical documentation.

04

Deliver & Review

Provide the agreed deliverables and review the results against the approved project scope.
