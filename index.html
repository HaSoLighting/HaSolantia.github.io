Portfolio & Educational Hub

Power quality & lighting design
knowledge and portfolio.

This website shares educational content and project work focused on electrical power quality (voltage stability, harmonics, grounding, surge risks), and architectural lighting design. It is designed to build knowledge, awareness, and future impact.

Non-commercial site — no services are offered or solicited here.

What you'll find on this site

Practical explanations, diagrams, and design examples — written to be clear, technical, and useful for students, homeowners, and professionals.

  • Power-quality basics: sags, surges, harmonics, grounding.
  • System concepts: AVR, SPD types, filtering, load behavior.
  • Lighting design: layers of light, glare, metrics, workflows.
  • Portfolio case studies (academic/personal projects).
