Portfolio & Educational Hub

Power quality & lighting design
knowledge and portfolio.

This website shares educational content and project work focused on electrical power system and architectural lighting design. It is built as a dual-track learning and portfolio platform for clear explanations, practical insight, and future impact.

Non-commercial site — no services are offered or solicited here.

What you will find on this site

Practical explanations, diagrams, and design examples — written to be clear, technical, and useful for students, homeowners, and professionals.

  • Power quality: voltage stability, surges, harmonics, grounding.
  • Lighting design: layers of light, glare, metrics, workflows.
  • Case studies and portfolio-ready academic/personal work.
  • Structured topics designed to grow into a full knowledge hub.
