Service Delivery

A defined process for every engagement

Project scopes are established before work begins so responsibilities, deliverables, schedule, assumptions, and limitations are clear to all parties.

01

Project Review

Review available drawings, files, technical information, project objectives, and requested services.

02

Scope Development

Define required inputs, deliverables, responsibilities, schedule, assumptions, and fee.

03

Technical Development

Complete the agreed simulation, analysis, research, design-support work, or documentation.

04

Delivery & Review

Deliver the agreed project materials and complete the review process defined in the project scope.
