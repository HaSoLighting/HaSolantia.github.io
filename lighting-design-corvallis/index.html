Lighting services

Lighting Design Support in Corvallis, Oregon

Based in Corvallis, HaSolantia helps project teams evaluate lighting before implementation. We provide architectural lighting design support, photometric analysis, and technical documentation for interior and exterior spaces, with remote support available beyond Oregon.

Photometric analysis and simulation

AGi32 and DIALux simulations help evaluate illuminance, uniformity, visual comfort, and light trespass. Analysis uses your drawings, luminaire specifications, manufacturer IES files, and agreed project criteria.

Photometric analysis services

Interior and exterior lighting support

Support for retail spaces, offices, multipurpose rooms, and parking areas includes conceptual layouts, luminaire comparisons, fixture schedules, and calculation reports. Scope and deliverables are agreed before work begins.

Lighting design support services

Research and technical documentation

Standards review, lighting research, data analysis, and technical reports help explain performance assumptions and support informed decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Can you help with a project outside Corvallis?

Yes. Remote simulation and documentation support can be scoped using drawings, specifications, and project information supplied by your team.

What will I receive?

Depending on scope, deliverables can include calculation grids, lighting layouts, fixture comparisons, rendered views, and a report describing assumptions and results.

Do these services include installation or stamped engineering?

HaSolantia provides analysis, research, documentation, and design support. Services requiring professional licensure must be performed or approved by an appropriately licensed professional.
