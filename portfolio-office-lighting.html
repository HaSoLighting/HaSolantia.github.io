Office Renovation Lighting Concept

A competition design case study for a multi-area office renovation, focused on transitions, identity, layered light, and balanced ambient levels.

Project snapshot
  • Project type: IES Brandston competition submission
  • Role: Concept development, lighting strategy, fixture research, visualization, and documentation
  • Spaces: Vestibules, lobbies, corridors, lounge, and multipurpose room
  • Deliverables: Concept boards, lighting plans, fixture schedules, and renderings
  • Considerations: Daylight integration, glare, visual comfort, atmosphere, and design identity
